Easy Researcher Library

Pick articles or books, then enter a DOI, title or ISBN. We look for a legal, free full-text copy. Results appear on this page; nothing is sent by email.

Sources: Crossref, Unpaywall, OpenAlex, Europe PMC, Semantic Scholar, Internet Archive, arXiv, DOAJ, CORE, Open Library, Google Books, Project Gutenberg, plus links to 100+ open repositories. Only links to copies lawfully shared by publishers, authors, and repositories.
